FOUNDER NOTE / ELHAM AMINI

Why I started
FYNX.

A simple purpose is a useful place to begin.

BY ELHAM AMINI

Founder of FYNX

About the author ↗

I started FYNX to help retail traders.

That is the starting point. It gives the work a clear audience and a practical question to return to: does this help someone understand what they are doing?

The work around the decision

Trading involves more than an entry and an exit. There is the preparation before a decision, the calculation of risk, the context of the market, and the review afterward. Those steps need useful tools and understandable explanations.

With FYNX, I’m building around that process. A calculator gives a number a structure. A lesson gives a concept context. A journal gives a decision a record that can be revisited.

Keep the purpose close

The wider vision for FYNX extends beyond one product. Finance World, the app, the API and the Journal express different parts of that direction. The roadmap gives the longer-term ambitions a place to be explored.

As the work grows, the original purpose remains the reference point: help retail traders. Make the tools useful. Give people a clearer place to start.

Elham Amini
