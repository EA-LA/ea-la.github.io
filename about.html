The starting point

For an independent trader, a lot of the work happens around the trade itself: understanding a market, preparing a calculation, thinking through risk, and coming back to review the decision.

FYNX brings tools and learning resources into that process. The mission is practical: give retail traders something useful to work with.

How I think about the work

Clarity, independence and long-term thinking connect the products. A calculator should make its inputs understandable. Research should make its sources visible. A journal should give a review somewhere to begin.

The trader’s own judgment stays at the center. Good tools can help someone ask better questions and build a process they understand.

Building beyond a single tool

FYNX includes Finance World, the iPhone app, developer APIs, the Journal and FYNX Funded. Each has its own role in the wider ecosystem.

The public roadmap explores the longer-term vision. It describes ambitions and direction; it does not promise dates or indicate that every proposed service is available today.
